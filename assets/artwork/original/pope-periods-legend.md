# Map legend: eras of the papacy

Source: Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Papacy" (https://www.britannica.com/topic/papacy). Its history is divided into these five sections:
- The early papacy: from St. Peter to 590 ce
- The medieval papacy: from 590 to 1303
- The Renaissance and Reformation papacy: from 1303 to 1565
- The early modern papacy: from 1566 to 1774
- The modern papacy: from 1775 to the 21st century

| Pin | Era (Britannica) | Years | First and last pope placed in it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold pin, bearded pope with the pallium | Early papacy | St Peter to 590 | St Peter to Pelagius II |
| Red pin, pope with the triple tiara | Medieval papacy | 590 to 1303 | St Gregory the Great to Boniface VIII |
| Sky blue pin, pope in a red camauro and gold stole | Renaissance and Reformation papacy | 1303 to 1565 | Benedict XI to Pius IV |
| Stone brown pin, pope in a red mozzetta and white zucchetto | Early modern papacy | 1566 to 1774 | St Pius V to Clement XIV |
| Navy pin, pope in white | Modern papacy | 1775 to today | Pius VI to Leo XIV |

Rule for placing a pope: use the year his pontificate began.

Alternative published division (six eras), from Eamon Duffy, Saints and Sinners: A History of the Popes (Yale University Press): "Upon this rock" c. AD 33-461; "Between two empires" 461-1000; "Set above nations" 1000-1447; "Protest and division" (printed as 1477-1774 in library catalogue contents, likely a misprint for 1447); "The pope and the people" 1774-1903; "The oracles of God" 1903 to the present.
